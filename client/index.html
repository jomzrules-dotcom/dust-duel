How to play

Two robot vacuums. One dirty house. One minute.

  • Clean mode: drive over dirt to make it your color. Your bin fills as you clean. Driving over the other player's floor steals it.
  • Dump mode: empty your bin behind you — it only lands on the other player's clean floor.
  • Full bin = you can't clean until you dump. Bumping knocks you both away.
  • Power-ups appear on the glowing spots. Hold one, use it when it counts.
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows to drive, Space / Enter switches Clean and Dump, Q / R Shift uses your item. Touch: left half is the joystick, round buttons on the right.
